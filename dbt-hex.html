dbt platform + Hex Enterprise

A field guide for account executives and sales engineers · By Minjae Lee

dbt is where a data team builds, tests and documents its logic. Hex is where people and agents use it. With the managed dbt platform, Hex reads that work directly: test results and freshness show up next to every table, and governed metrics are queryable with no SQL. Together they give an enterprise agent rollout a foundation the data team can stand behind.

03Why dbt matters to Hex

Hex agents are only as good as the context behind them. dbt is where most of that context is born: the models, the tests that prove them, the descriptions that explain them, and the metrics that define the business. The dbt platform turns that work into APIs Hex can read. The stronger the dbt project, the better every Hex agent answers.

04Discovery questions for a joint deal

  1. Are you on dbt Core or the dbt platform today, and who runs your orchestration?
  2. When someone in Hex picks a table, how do they know it passed its tests and is up to date?
  3. Where do your metric definitions live, and how many tools redefine them?
  4. You are rolling out agents. What do they use as the source of truth for metrics?
  5. Who owns data quality when an agent gives the business a wrong number?
