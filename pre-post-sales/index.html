Every software sale has two halves. Pre-sales is before the deal closes: someone has to show a customer the product can solve their problem. Post-sales is after the deal closes: someone has to make sure it actually does.

I've done both. This is why that makes me better at pre-sales, and why doing both, from pre-sales through post-sales, is where I'm headed.
